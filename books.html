The novels

Books

Four novels, published between 2019 and 2026. Three stand alone. The fourth opens a series.

  • March 2026 · The Athenaeum Files, Book One

    The Unraveling of August Finch

    When charismatic museum archivist August Finch vanishes during a blackout at the Boston Athenaeum, he leaves behind a clean desk, a locked safe, and a single page from a nineteenth-century ledger that shouldn’t exist. His estranged daughter Maya — a forensic accountant who deals exclusively in hard numbers — must follow a trail of meticulously staged historical anomalies left specifically for her.

  • 2023 · Standalone

    A Short History of the Duplicate

    In an alternate 1980s London, citizens can apply for a legal “Shadow” — a silent synthetic double designed to absorb their grief, stress and public failures. Julian, a meticulous clerk in the Ministry of Records, has lived alongside his for twelve quiet years. Then his duplicate begins making decisions of its own.

  • 2021 · Standalone

    The Guest List for the End of the World

    Nora was hired to plan the social event of the decade: a hyper-exclusive ten-day retreat on a private Icelandic island for eight tech moguls and their inner circles. When communication with the mainland quietly goes dark on day three, the guests are left to wonder whether it is part of the experience — or whether the world outside has actually fallen apart.

  • 2019 · Standalone · Debut novel

    The Weight of Unspoken Tides

    Set along the eroding bluffs of a northern coastal town, this debut traces three generations of a lighthouse-keeping family confronting the literal and figurative shifting of their ground. When the youngest son, an acoustic ecologist, returns home to record the dying underwater hums of the local reef, he uncovers decades of suppressed family journals tucked beneath the floorboards.
