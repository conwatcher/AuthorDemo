The new novel

The Unraveling of August Finch

The Athenaeum Files · Book One · March 2026

Some legacies are buried. Others are left as clues.

When charismatic museum archivist August Finch vanishes during a blackout at the Boston Athenaeum, he leaves behind a clean desk, a locked safe, and a single page from a nineteenth-century ledger that shouldn’t exist. His estranged daughter Maya — a forensic accountant who deals exclusively in hard numbers — is forced to step into her father’s world of missing artifacts and polished secrets.

To track him down, she must follow a trail of meticulously staged historical anomalies left specifically for her, forcing her to calculate the exact cost of the lies that held her family together.

The work

Four novels about inheritance, evidence, and the stories families agree to tell

T.R. Dailey writes quiet books with something sharp underneath them. A lighthouse family sorting through journals they were never meant to read. Eight billionaires on an island where the radios have gone silent. A clerk in an alternate London whose synthetic double has started making plans of its own.

What ties them together is not a genre so much as a question: what happens to the truth once everyone involved has agreed to leave it alone?
